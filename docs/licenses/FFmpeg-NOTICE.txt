FFmpeg 9.0.1 is dynamically linked under LGPL 2.1 or later.
The license is included in FFmpeg-LGPL-2.1.txt. Corresponding source and
vcpkg build patches/recipe are in FFmpeg-9.0.1-corresponding-source.zip.
Build with the included vcpkg manifest and port on Windows x64 (MSVC).
The shared FFmpeg DLLs in the engine directory may be replaced by compatible
modified builds. Reverse engineering for debugging modifications to these
LGPL libraries is permitted. No GPL or nonfree features are enabled.
Upstream: https://ffmpeg.org/ ; source tag: n9.0.1.

The editor also ships ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe, with libavfilter,
libswscale and libswresample. Encoding uses NVIDIA NVENC or Windows Media
Foundation for H.264 and FFmpeg's LGPL AAC encoder. PNG support links zlib;
its notice is included as zlib-LICENSE.txt. No libx264 or GPL filters are used.
